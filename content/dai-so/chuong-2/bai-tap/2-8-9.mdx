$$
M_{11}=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{7}{10}&- \frac{3}{10}\\- \frac{1}{10}&\frac{3}{5}\end{bmatrix},\qquad C_{11}=(-1)^{1+1}\det M_{11}=\frac{39}{100}.
