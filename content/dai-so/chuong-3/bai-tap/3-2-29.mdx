<GoiY so={2}>

<LoiGiai>

Vì $\operatorname{adj}A=2A^{-1}$, có $A^{-1}+4\operatorname{adj}A=9A^{-1}$. Cấp 3 cho $\det(9A^{-1})=9^3/\det A=729/2$.

</LoiGiai>
